# Can I put a TV remote widget on my Android home screen?

Yes. The Remote has home-screen widgets for Android phones. The My TV widget shows your TV's name and room with an Open remote button. The Your TVs widget lists your saved TVs with their rooms, like Living Room TV and Bedroom TV.

## The My TV widget: your TV and an Open remote button

The My TV widget shows one saved TV, for example "Living Room TV" with its room "Living room", and a large **Open remote** button.

## The Your TVs widget: every saved TV with its room

The Your TVs widget shows how many TVs you've picked for it and a button for each TV with its name and room, for example "Living Room TV, Living room" and "Bedroom TV, Bedroom".

## How to add a widget on Android

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen.
2. Tap **Widgets**.
3. Find **The Remote** and pick the widget you want.
4. Drag it onto your home screen.

The exact steps depend on your phone's launcher.

## The widgets show the TVs you've saved

The widgets show the TVs you've saved in The Remote, with the names and rooms you gave them. To add a TV, pick it in the app and enter the code it shows.

## Where The Remote fits

The Remote is a free phone remote for Android TV and Google TV, with no ads and no account. Search YouTube from your phone and the results open on the TV. Share a YouTube link and it plays on the TV. Your phone needs Android 8.0 or later. Your TV needs Android TV Remote Service v2, on the same home Wi-Fi.
