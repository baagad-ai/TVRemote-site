# The YouTube keyboard on Android TV is slow. Is there a quicker way?

Yes. Type the search on your phone instead of the TV. The Remote is a free Android app with its own YouTube search: you type on your phone keyboard, and the results open in YouTube on your Android TV or Google TV. It has no ads and no sign-up.

## Why the TV keyboard feels slow

In YouTube on the TV, you move a highlight across a grid of letters with the remote and press OK on each one. A long title, a typo or a name you're not sure how to spell means a lot of back and forth. That's fine for a word or two. For anything longer, your phone keyboard is easier.

## Type the search on your phone instead

1. Open The Remote and pick the TV.
2. Tap Search YouTube.
3. Type your search on your phone keyboard. Fix any typos there, then send it.
4. The results open in YouTube on the TV.

The first time, the TV may ask which app to use. Choose YouTube.

## If you already have the video, skip the search

If a friend sent the video, or you found it on your phone, you don't need to search at all. Share the link to The Remote and it plays on the TV. See [how to send a YouTube link to your TV](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/app-to-send-youtube-link-to-tv/).

## Why not type into YouTube's box with a phone keyboard?

Some phone remotes type into whichever text box the TV has selected. On some TVs, YouTube's search box doesn't accept that text, so you're back on the letter grid. The [phone keyboard guide](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/why-wont-my-phone-keyboard-type-in-youtube-on-android-tv/) explains what to check.

## Other ways round the keyboard

If your TV remote has a voice button, pressing it while you're in an app searches within that app (see [Android TV Help](https://support.google.com/androidtv/answer/6121377?hl=en)). YouTube's phone app can also send videos to the TV. Both are covered in [searching YouTube on your TV with your Android phone](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/search-youtube-tv-android-phone/).

## Pick the right TV first

If you've saved more than one TV, check which one is selected before you send the search. You can name your TVs and sort them by room, like Living room or Bedroom, so the results open on the screen you meant.

## What you need

- An Android phone with Android 8.0 or later. There's no iPhone version.
- An Android TV or Google TV with Android TV Remote Service v2, on the same home Wi-Fi as your phone.
- YouTube on the TV. Search support depends on the TV's YouTube app version.

The Remote's phone typing is for YouTube search only. It doesn't type into other TV apps.
