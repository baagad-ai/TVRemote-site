# Lost your Chromecast with Google TV remote? What to do

Chromecast with Google TV doesn't have buttons for menus, so you need another controller until the remote turns up or a replacement arrives. Here are the routes, starting with the ones you already have.

## Use your phone as the remote

If the Chromecast is already set up on your home Wi-Fi:

1. Switch the TV to the Chromecast's HDMI input.
2. Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Open the Google TV app, tap the remote or connect button, choose the Chromecast, and enter the code shown on the TV.

The Google Home app can also open a remote for the device. [Google's phone remote steps](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/11221499?hl=en) If the TV code is new to you, see [What is the code my TV shows when I pair my phone?](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/tv-pairing-code-when-connecting-phone/).

## Try your TV's own remote (HDMI-CEC)

Many TVs can pass their remote's buttons to an HDMI device through HDMI-CEC. TV makers give it different names, such as BRAVIA Sync (Sony), Anynet+ (Samsung), SimpLink (LG) and EasyLink (Philips). Turn it on in the TV's settings if it's off. Google notes the Chromecast must be powered from a wall outlet, not the TV's USB port, for CEC features to work. [Google's CEC page](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/7199917?hl=en)

Which buttons pass through depends on the TV, so treat this as a stopgap rather than a full remote.

## Pair a replacement Voice Remote

Google sells replacement Voice Remotes. To pair one with a Chromecast that's already set up:

1. Turn on the Chromecast.
2. Press the button on the back of the Chromecast for 5 to 10 seconds to start pairing mode.
3. Put AAA batteries in the new remote. It usually pairs on its own; if not, hold **Back** and **Home** on the remote.

[Google's replacement remote steps](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/10089168?hl=en) Then set up its volume and power buttons for your TV under Settings → Remotes & Accessories. [Google's button setup](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/10094150?hl=en)

## If the Chromecast is on a setup screen or a new Wi-Fi

A phone remote needs the Chromecast on your home network first. If you moved house or changed the router, see [Lost your Android TV remote?](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/lost-android-tv-remote/) for the Wi-Fi loop and what can break it. Avoid a factory reset as a shortcut: Google warns it erases the device, and setup can still need a remote.

## Common questions

### Can I control Chromecast with Google TV without the remote?

Yes, once it's on your home Wi-Fi. Use a phone remote such as the Google TV app, or your TV's remote if HDMI-CEC is on and your TV passes the buttons through.

### How do I pair a new remote to Chromecast with Google TV?

Press the button on the back of the Chromecast for 5 to 10 seconds, then put batteries in the new remote. If it doesn't pair on its own, hold Back and Home on the remote.

### Does my TV remote work with Chromecast with Google TV?

Often, through HDMI-CEC, but it depends on the TV. Turn CEC on in the TV's settings and power the Chromecast from a wall outlet.
