# How can guests control my Google TV without a Google account?

Invite them from The Remote. A guest joins on your home Wi-Fi with a short invite code from your phone, then controls the TV from their own Android phone, with no account and without your phone open.

## Chromecast guest mode is no longer supported

Google's help page says it plainly: "Guest mode is no longer supported. Guests can cast content from their Android or iOS devices by connecting to the host's Wi-Fi network." [Google: guest mode](https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/6109286) That old feature let people cast to older Chromecasts without joining your Wi-Fi. It ended in 2023, and it was never part of Chromecast with Google TV. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2023/08/23/chromecast-guest-mode/) So guests can still cast once they're on your Wi-Fi.

## Invite a guest from your phone

Any phone you've paired with the TV, using the code the TV shows, is a Primary remote for that TV. A Primary remote invites guests: open the TV's Guest access list in The Remote and tap Invite. Your guest enters the invite code on their phone. They can scan the QR code with their phone's camera or type the code.

Invites work on your home Wi-Fi only. The invite code works once, within 2 minutes. Guest access never expires.

If a TV doesn't accept the shared guest key, The Remote switches just that guest to their own TV code, which the TV shows once.

## Guests join your home Wi-Fi, not a guest network

Router guest networks often keep devices apart, so a guest on one may not find the TV. Put your guest on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. The [can't-find-your-TV guide](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/android-tv-remote-app-cant-find-tv/) covers the network checks.

Your guest needs The Remote on an Android phone (Android 8.0 or later), downloaded from this site. There's no iPhone version, and they don't need an account.

## Guests get the full remote, even when your phone is put away

By default, a guest gets everything on the remote: the arrows, media keys, volume, YouTube search typed on their phone, link sharing, Power and the apps list. They control the TV from their own phone, so yours can stay in your pocket.

Guests can't manage the TV. They can't rename it, change its room, manage guests or unpair it.

You can limit a guest to **Media and volume only**. That allows only play/pause, volume and mute. The limit is set per guest.

## See and remove guests

Each TV has its own guest list. Every Primary remote can invite and remove guests.

Tap Remove to take a guest off the list. You can do this any time. Removal is done by The Remote, not in the TV's own settings.

## Guests who stop visiting drop off by themselves

Guest access never expires. The Remote removes a guest by itself only after they've been off your home Wi-Fi and haven't connected for 7 days in a row. You can still remove anyone yourself whenever you like.

That includes Google TV devices that support the Android TV Remote Service, such as Chromecast with Google TV and Google TV Streamer.
