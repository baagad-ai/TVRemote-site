# How do I let friends control my Google TV at a party?

Invite them from The Remote on your phone. Each friend joins on your home Wi-Fi with a short invite code, then controls the TV from their own Android phone. Nobody has to pass your phone around, and nobody needs an account.

## Before people arrive

Your phone needs to be a Primary remote for the TV. That's any phone you've paired with the TV using the code the TV shows. If you haven't done that yet, see [what the code on your TV is for](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/tv-pairing-code-when-connecting-phone/).

Each friend needs The Remote on an Android phone (Android 8.0 or later), downloaded from this site. There's no iPhone version, so friends with iPhones can't join as guests.

## Invite friends as they get there

Open the TV's Guest access list in The Remote and tap Invite. Your friend enters the invite code on their phone.

They can scan the QR code with their phone's camera or type the code.

Invites work on your home Wi-Fi only, so your friend needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. An unused invite code expires after 2 minutes, and it's only used for joining.

A TV can have up to 10 guests.

## What your friends can do

By default, a guest gets the full remote: the arrows, media keys, volume, Power and the apps list. They can type a YouTube search on their phone and share links to the TV, so whoever picked the next song can put it on.

Guests can't manage the TV. They can't rename it, change its room, manage guests or unpair it. Your setup stays the way you left it.

## Give someone just the volume

If a friend only needs to turn it up or pause it, you can limit that guest to **Media and volume only**. That allows only play/pause, volume and mute. The limit is set per guest, so everyone else keeps the full remote.

## When the party's over

Open the TV's Guest access list and tap Remove next to anyone you want off it. Removal is done by The Remote, not in the TV's own settings. If a friend has already gone home, their entry shows Removal pending until both phones are on your Wi-Fi again.

You don't have to clean up the list. Guests who stop coming around are removed over time. Every Primary remote can invite and remove guests, so anyone else at home with their own Primary remote can tidy the list too.

## No server, no account

The invite goes from your phone to your friend's phone over your home Wi-Fi. There's no server in between and no account for you or them. If you'd rather send the invite as a link, the app can make one that you share yourself, and it expires after 2 minutes. More on that in [using a TV remote app without an account](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/tv-remote-app-without-account/).

For the full details on invites and the guest list, see [how Guest access works](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/guests-control-google-tv-without-google-account/).

The Remote is free, with no ads, for Android TV and Google TV devices with Android TV Remote Service v2. If someone's phone can't find the TV, the [can't-find-your-TV guide](https://theremote-site.pages.dev/guides/android-tv-remote-app-cant-find-tv/) covers the network checks.
