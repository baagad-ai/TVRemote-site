How to control a TCL Google TV without the remote

By The Remote · Sources reviewed 24 October 2026

If your TCL Google TV remote is lost or dead, you still have a few ways in. Which one works depends on whether the TV is already on your home Wi-Fi.

Use the button on the TV

TCL says the TV's own button is usually underneath or behind the TCL logo area. It's handy for power and checking the TV responds, but it isn't a comfortable way to move around Google TV. TCL's remote troubleshooting Check your model's manual for what the button can do.

Use your phone (the TV must be on your Wi-Fi)

Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

  • Google TV app (Android): open the app, tap the remote or connect button, choose the TCL TV and enter the code shown on screen. Google's phone remote steps
  • Google Home app: TCL documents adding the TV in Google Home and opening its remote. TCL notes you need to be signed in to the same Google account on the TV and the app. TCL's Google Home steps

TCL also notes that power control over the network may not work on every setup, so you may still need the TV's button to switch it on.

If your phone doesn't see the TV, check both are on your main home Wi-Fi network. More checks: Android TV remote app can't find your TV?

If the remote is there but not responding

Try fresh batteries, clear anything blocking the bottom front of the TV, and restart the TV by unplugging it. TCL's guide also shows how to check the remote's IR signal with a phone camera. See Google TV remote not working? for the full order.

Pair a replacement TCL remote

TCL sells Google TV remotes for its TVs. To pair one, stand near the TV and hold Home and OK on the remote together until the pairing animation appears. TCL's pairing steps Check the remote is listed for your exact model before buying.

If the TV isn't on your Wi-Fi

A phone remote can't reach a TV that's on a setup screen or an old network. See Lost your Android TV remote? for the options that don't depend on Wi-Fi, such as Ethernet or a USB keyboard on supported models.

Common questions

Can I use my phone as a remote for my TCL Google TV?

Yes. With the TV and phone on the same Wi-Fi, pair the Google TV app using the code on the TV, or open the TV's remote in the Google Home app.

How do I pair a new remote to my TCL Google TV?

Stand near the TV and hold Home and OK on the remote together until the pairing animation appears.

Where is the button on a TCL TV?

TCL says it's usually underneath or behind the TCL logo area. Check your model's manual for what it controls.
