Trackpad gestures in The Remote: what does each one do?

By The Remote · Sources reviewed 16 October 2026

The trackpad turns your phone's screen into a full-screen pad for your Android TV or Google TV. Tap for OK, swipe to move, and use two or three fingers for Back, Play/Pause and Home. It works over the same TV connection as the rest of the remote.

Every gesture at a glance

GestureWhat it does on the TV
TapOK
Swipe up, down, left or rightMoves one step that way
Swipe, then holdKeep your finger still at the end and it keeps moving that way, one step at a time. Lift to stop. If Repeat is off in Settings, each swipe is one step.
Two-finger tapBack
Your phone's back gestureBack
Two-finger swipe leftSkip back (varies by app)
Two-finger swipe rightSkip forward (varies by app)
Three-finger tapPlay/Pause
Three-finger swipe upHome
Your phone's volume keysTV volume, while the pad is open

One swipe is one step

Each swipe moves one step, however fast or far you swipe. To keep moving along a long row, swipe and hold at the end. Lift your finger to stop.

Back, Play/Pause and Home use more fingers

Tap with two fingers for Back. Your phone's own back gesture also sends Back to the TV while the pad is open. A three-finger tap is Play/Pause, and a three-finger swipe up anywhere on the pad goes Home.

Skip back and forward depend on the app

A two-finger swipe left sends skip back, and a two-finger swipe right sends skip forward (both vary by app). The app playing on the TV decides how far it jumps, and whether it responds at all.

Your phone's volume keys control the TV

While the pad is open, your phone's volume keys change the TV's volume.

The only button is Exit

While connected, the pad has one button: Exit, in the corner. Tap it to go back to the buttons. The rest of the screen is pad.

Help and gesture settings are on the Gestures screen

The first time you open the pad, a short practice coach shows the main gestures. After that, help is on the Gestures screen, along with remapping.

You can remap gestures, within limits

On the Gestures screen you can move actions to other gestures. You can only choose from the remote's own actions, such as OK, Back, Home, Play/Pause and skip back/forward. Power is never a gesture, so a stray swipe can't turn the TV off.

If the TV disconnects

The pad greys out and shows "Reconnecting to Living Room TV…", with your TV's name. Gestures pause until the TV is back. If the TV is asleep, the pad shows the same card as the button remote.

What you need

The Remote is free, with no ads and no account. It runs on Android phones with Android 8.0 or later (no iPhone version). Your TV needs Android TV Remote Service v2, on the same home Wi-Fi as your phone. Available controls vary by TV model and app. Prefer bigger buttons instead? See Larger controls and Easy remote.
