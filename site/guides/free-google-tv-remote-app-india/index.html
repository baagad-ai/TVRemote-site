Which free phone remote apps work with Google TV in India?

By The Remote · Sources reviewed 11 October 2026

The free options are Google's Google TV app and Google Home app, your TV maker's own app if it has one, and The Remote. The Remote is a free Android remote with no ads and no account. Its app language list includes हिन्दी, ಕನ್ನಡ, বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు and मराठी, alongside English.

Google's own apps

The Google TV app works as a phone remote for Android TV and Google TV. You pair it with the code your TV shows, on the same Wi-Fi. Google TV Help Google Home can also act as a remote for Google's streaming devices. Google Streaming Help

Your TV maker's app

Some brands have their own app. Sony's BRAVIA Connect, for example, supports listed BRAVIA models. Sony: BRAVIA Connect or Google TV app Check your TV brand's support page.

The Remote: no ads, no account, Indian app languages

  • Free, with no ads, no sign-up and no in-app purchases.
  • Search YouTube by typing on your phone. The results open on the TV.
  • Share a YouTube link, from WhatsApp for example, and it plays on the TV.
  • Name your TVs and organise them by room.
  • App language choices on the language screen: English, हिन्दी, ಕನ್ನಡ, বাংলা, தமிழ், తెలుగు, मराठी and more. The screen says languages that aren't available use English.

Installing The Remote

The Remote installs from our website as an APK, not from an app store. Here's how installing from a website works. Your phone needs Android 8.0 or later. There's no iPhone version.

Which TVs

Android TV and Google TV devices with Android TV Remote Service v2. That includes Google TV devices that support the Android TV Remote Service, such as Chromecast with Google TV and Google TV Streamer. Your phone and TV need the same home Wi-Fi.
