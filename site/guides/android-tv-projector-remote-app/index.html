Lost your projector remote? Phone control for Android TV projectors

By The Remote · Sources reviewed 24 October 2026

A phone remote app can control a projector only if the projector runs Android TV or Google TV and is on your home Wi-Fi. Many projectors run other systems or a plain Android build, so check that first.

1. Check what the projector runs

Look at the projector's box, manual or product page for Android TV or Google TV. A projector that says "Android" without either name may not include the service phone remotes use. These guides help:

  • How do I know if my TV is Android TV or Google TV?
  • How do I check if my TV has Android TV Remote Service?

If the projector maker has its own app, its support pages are the best place to start. For example, XGIMI's help centre answers this for some of its projectors. XGIMI help centre

2. Pair a phone remote

With the projector on and on the same Wi-Fi as your phone:

  1. Open the Google TV app on your Android phone and tap the remote or connect button.
  2. Choose the projector and enter the code it shows.

Google's phone remote steps If the projector doesn't appear, see Android TV remote app can't find your TV?.

3. If the projector isn't on your Wi-Fi

A phone remote can't reach a projector on a setup screen or an old network. Check for buttons on the projector body, an Ethernet port, or a USB keyboard if the manual says it's supported. Lost your Android TV remote? covers the Wi-Fi loop in more detail.

4. Get the right replacement

Projector remotes are often model-specific. Use the model number from the label and the maker's support or parts pages before buying a generic remote.

Common questions

Can I control my projector with my phone?

If it runs Android TV or Google TV and is on the same Wi-Fi as your phone, you can usually pair a phone remote with the code it shows. Other systems need the maker's own app or remote.

Why can't my phone remote app find my projector?

The projector may not run Android TV or Google TV, may lack Android TV Remote Service, or may be on a different network from your phone.

What if my projector is on a setup screen?

A phone remote can't help until the projector is on your network. Use buttons on the projector, Ethernet, a supported USB keyboard, or a replacement remote.
