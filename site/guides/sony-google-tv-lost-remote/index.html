Lost your Sony Google TV remote? How to find or replace it

By The Remote · Sources reviewed 24 October 2026

Some Sony BRAVIA Google TV remotes can make a sound to help you find them. If yours can't, or it's gone for good, your phone can run the TV until a replacement arrives.

Make the remote sound (supported models only)

Sony's Find remote feature works only with supplied remotes that have speaker holes on the back and a built-in buzzer.

  1. Press the Power button on the TV itself. A menu appears on screen.
  2. Select Find remote. The remote should buzz.

Press any button on the remote to stop it. The option only appears when a buzzer remote is paired, and the signal has a limited range. Sony lists which models and remotes support it. Sony's Find remote article

Control the TV from your phone

Sony documents two phone options. Both need the TV and phone on the same Wi-Fi and the TV signed in to a Google account. Sony's phone remote article

  • Google TV app: install it, choose the TV it finds, and enter the pairing code shown on the TV.
  • BRAVIA Connect app: supported on a limited list of recent BRAVIA models, and it asks you to sign in with a Sony account.

If the phone can't find the TV, see Android TV remote app can't find your TV?.

Use the TV's buttons

Sony's troubleshooting suggests checking the buttons on the TV itself; their position varies by model, so look at the sides, back, front and top. Sony's remote troubleshooting Some Sony models also recognise USB keyboards. Sony's keyboard guidance

Get a replacement

Use the model number on the TV's label and Sony's support pages to find a remote listed for your model. A remote for a different BRAVIA year may not offer the same features. If your remote is present but not responding, try Google TV remote not working? first.

Common questions

Can I make my Sony remote beep to find it?

Only on supported models with a buzzer remote (speaker holes on the back). Press the TV's Power button and choose Find remote from the on-screen menu.

Can I control my Sony BRAVIA with my phone?

Yes, with the TV and phone on the same Wi-Fi. Sony documents the Google TV app and, on some recent models, its BRAVIA Connect app.

Which replacement remote do I need?

Check the model number on your TV's label and pick a remote Sony lists for that model.
