Google TV remote not working? Fixes to try, in order

By The Remote · Sources reviewed 24 October 2026

Most remote problems on Google TV come down to three things: power, pairing, or the infrared (IR) signal used for TV power and volume. Work through the checks below in order. Your TV maker's steps come first if they differ.

1. Rule out the batteries

Take both batteries out, put them back the right way round, and watch the remote's light. Google's steps for its own Voice Remote say the light should flash twice; if it doesn't, try the original batteries or fresh AAA batteries, then take them out for a few minutes and try again. Google's Voice Remote troubleshooting

On a TV with a built-in Google TV, such as a Sony or TCL model, use the maker's guide. Sony suggests removing the batteries, holding the remote's Power button, then reinserting them. Sony's remote troubleshooting

2. Restart the TV or streaming device

Unplug the TV or streamer from the wall, wait, and plug it back in. This clears many temporary faults, and it doesn't erase anything. Avoid a factory reset as a first step: it erases the device and you may need a working remote to set it up again.

3. Only volume or power works

If the remote changes the volume but can't move around the menus, navigation and volume are using different routes. Volume and power on the TV usually go over IR, while menu navigation needs the remote to be paired with the Google TV device.

  • Re-pair the remote. On Chromecast with Google TV, Google's steps are to forget the remote under Settings → Remotes & Accessories, then pair again. On TCL Google TV, TCL says to hold Home and OK together near the TV until pairing starts. TCL's pairing steps
  • If navigation works but volume or power doesn't, check the remote button setup under Settings → Remotes & Accessories → Set up remote buttons, and point the remote at the TV. Google's volume and power setup

You need some way to move around the menus to re-pair. If the remote can't do it, pair your phone first (below), then use it to reach the remote settings.

4. Check the IR signal and the TV sensor

TCL suggests pointing a phone camera at the tip of the remote while pressing Power: a flashing light on the camera screen means the remote is sending IR. Also move a soundbar or anything else that blocks the front of the TV. TCL's remote troubleshooting

5. Update the remote

Google TV can offer remote updates under Settings → Remotes & Accessories → your remote. Google's Google TV fixes Sony has a separate remote software update article for its Android TV and Google TV models.

Keep the TV usable while you fix it

If the TV is already on your home Wi-Fi, your phone can stand in. Google's own Google TV app pairs with the code shown on your TV. Google's phone remote steps If your phone can't find the TV, see Android TV remote app can't find your TV?.

If the remote is dead for good, buy a replacement confirmed for your exact model, or read Lost your Android TV remote? for other routes.

Common questions

Why does only the volume work on my Google TV remote?

Volume and power usually use the IR signal, while menu navigation needs the remote to be paired with the Google TV device. Re-pair the remote from Settings → Remotes & Accessories, or with your TV maker's pairing buttons.

Will a factory reset fix my remote?

Try batteries, a restart and re-pairing first. A factory reset erases the device, and setting it up again may need a working remote.

Can I use my phone while the remote is broken?

Yes, if the TV is on the same home Wi-Fi as your phone. Pair the Google TV app, or another phone remote for Android TV and Google TV, with the code shown on the TV.
