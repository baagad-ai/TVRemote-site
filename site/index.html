COMPATIBLE ANDROID TV

Connect over
your home network.

The Remote works with compatible Android TV and Google TV devices that support Android TV Remote Service v2. Your phone and TV pair over your home network; the app does not require an account or send remote commands through a developer-operated cloud relay.

Compatibility and available controls vary by TV. YouTube search and playback also depend on the TV model and YouTube version.

COMPATIBILITY, SEARCH & PRIVACY

Before you connect.

Which TVs can I use?

The app is for compatible Android TV and Google TV devices that support Android TV Remote Service v2. TV support and available controls can vary by model.

Do I need a TV companion for YouTube search?

No. For YouTube search, the phone sends the search to the selected TV over your home network. Support varies by TV model and YouTube version. Other supported TV searches use the optional TV companion.

Does it send my search to a server?

The search goes to the TV you selected over your local network. YouTube may process a YouTube search link under its privacy policy. The Remote does not keep a search history.

Can I join the beta now?

Not yet. Google Play beta access is not open. This page will link to the official opt-in when it is ready. No signup details are collected here.
