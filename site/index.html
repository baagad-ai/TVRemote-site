BEFORE YOU PAIR

A few clear answers.

Which TVs does it work with?

The app is for compatible Android TV and Google TV devices that support Android TV Remote Service v2. TV support and available controls can vary by model.

Does YouTube search need a TV companion?

No. YouTube search uses a direct phone-to-TV route. Support varies by TV model and YouTube version. Other supported TV searches use the optional TV companion.

Does it send my search to a server?

The search is sent to the TV you selected over your local network. YouTube may process the YouTube search link under its own privacy policy. The Remote does not keep a search history.

Can I join the beta now?

Not yet from this page. Beta access is being set up; the button will become active when the official Play opt-in is ready. This page does not collect signup details.
