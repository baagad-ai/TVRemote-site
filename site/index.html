The familiar remote controls, plus useful shortcuts for the way you watch.

01

Straight to YouTube search

Type on your phone. One dedicated action opens the TV’s YouTube search route and sends your query, without navigating to its on-screen keyboard.

02

A remote that knows the app

App-aware profiles follow the app your TV reports. You can also choose a profile yourself, keeping the remote suited to what you’re watching.

BEFORE YOU PAIR

A few clear answers.

Which TVs does it work with?

The app is for compatible Android TV and Google TV devices that support Android TV Remote Service v2. TV support and available controls can vary by model.

Does YouTube search need a TV companion?

No. YouTube search uses a direct phone-to-TV route. Support varies by TV model and YouTube version. Other supported search providers use the optional TV companion.

Can I join the beta now?

Not yet from this page. Beta access is being set up; the button will become active when the official Play opt-in is ready. The beta does not collect signup details here.
